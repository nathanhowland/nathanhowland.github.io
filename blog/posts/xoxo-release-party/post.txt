Last week I played an album release party for "XOXO," the album Alaska-303 and I had been working on for the last several months.

[image: nathan-and-alaska.jpg | Me and Alaska.]

We had played a show a couple months earlier for the release of "Engine," a single from the album, at a local dive bar in Canton. It was a blast, but some amateurish elements of the stage and sound system made the level of production we were hoping for difficult to reach.

[image: flyer.jpg | Show flyer.]

Alaska reached out to No Class after our first show because we wanted a more solid venue to debut our music at. No Class is equipped with an absolutely solid stereo system, fat subs, and a professional lighting setup. The sound and lighting tech who worked our show, Finley (@girlgonemiss.wav on IG), was phenomenal and brought our performance up a level with her lighting operator skills.

[image: setlist.jpg | Setlist for the evening.]

[instagram: https://www.instagram.com/p/Dbv41QCBXT3/ | Filmed by @starboy.images.]

Our show was a hit! We had 70-plus people pull up for the debut. A lot of them were friends, but another big chunk were people Alaska authentically gathered to the show through their social media presence. It was a trip to see so many people excited by the music we made together. For this set I DJ'd the tracks and performed mild ambient in between sets and songs. I also ran sound for the various drag performers we had with us and a fantastic local rapper, 24Megabytes, who opened. DJ Pink Chao also provided dance music before and after the performances.
